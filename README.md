## 👤 about me

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yasameeen/yasameeen/main/assets/about.svg" width="100%" alt="Teacher first, engineer now. I build education technology that reaches 600k+ active students across Brazil and Panama.">

I am a **product engineer** working where education meets technology. I started in the classroom — teaching English at CNA+ — and moved into engineering at **Expandel**, where I build AI, data and full-stack products that schools in Brazil and Latin America run every day.

**What I am going after:** AI in education that is obvious to use, truly inclusive, and measurable in learning outcomes. I want to keep working with people who treat education as infrastructure, not as a market.

## 📈 my results

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yasameeen/yasameeen/main/assets/results.svg" width="100%" alt="Results: 600k+ active users, education systems aligned with MEC Brazil and MEDUCA Panama, hardened anti-cheat on offline ENEM mock exams, number one device and print management choice">

- **600k+ active users** across every platform I work on.
- **Truly inclusive education** — schools that depend on this software every single day.
- **Anti-cheat that holds.** I hardened an offline **ENEM mock-exam** system against cheating: it detects suspicious activity from simple keyboard commands to **F12 / devtools** being open, and tracks the mandatory fullscreen exit during the exam.
- **AI aligned with curriculum.** I helped build an AI education platform aligned with **MEC (Brazil)** and **MEDUCA (Panama)**, for students and teachers — it received **Google's Editors' Choice** seal and is praised by State Departments of Education. I also ran training sessions and presentations on it.
- **Fleet at school scale.** I support **Windows, Android and ChromeOS device management** and **print management** platforms that have won public tenders (pregões) and remain our clients' **#1 choice across Brazil**.
- **Innovation from the field.** My experience and presence at education events let me bring innovation and address educators' real pain points in a careful, attentive way — always inside **LGPD** and **MEC** guidelines.
